SB or FinCom not “Approve”: Art. 12, 13, 14
At a glance
  • One session, 14 articles. Monday, November 9 at 7:00 PM in the Medway High School Auditorium, 88 Summer Street. Warrant signed by the Select Board on October 23, 2026.
  • Free Cash is the big money story. Articles 2–5 move about $4.27M out of Certified Free Cash — $4.0M of it into the Capital Stabilization Fund (Art. 2).
  • Two citizens' petitions on license plate readers (Arts. 12–13). The Select Board recommends Disapprove on both; the Finance Committee takes No Position on Art. 12 and recommends Disapprove on Art. 13.
  • Finance Committee recommendation still open on Art. 14 ($1.04M raised on the tax levy for debt service and revaluation) — listed as To Be Determined in the warrant.
  • Higher vote thresholds. Four zoning articles (9, 10, 11, 13) generally need a two-thirds vote. Paying prior-year bills (Art. 1) needs nine-tenths at a meeting other than the Annual Town Meeting.

Art. 9
Zoning: Nonconforming Uses and Structures
Updates Section 5.5 to match recent changes to the state Zoning Act (G.L. c. 40A). Key effects: permits stay protected from later zoning changes for 24 months instead of 12; homes that are nonconforming only because of lot size, frontage, coverage or floor-area ratio can be expanded as of right (no ZBA finding) if the addition meets current height, story and setback rules; and an unused nonconforming use keeps its status for four years instead of two.
SB: ApproveFinCom: Approve2/3 vote
Sponsor: Planning & Economic Development Board
 Zoning —
Art. 10
Zoning: Trail Signs
Creates a sign category for trails and multi-use paths in residential districts. Defines a “Trail Sign,” exempts small trailhead, wayfinding and trail-marker signs from sign permits, and allows one freestanding (up to 40 sq. ft., 6 ft tall) and one projecting sign per trail parking area. DCR-run trails are excluded.
SB: ApproveFinCom: Approve2/3 vote
Sponsor: Planning & Economic Development Board
 Zoning —
